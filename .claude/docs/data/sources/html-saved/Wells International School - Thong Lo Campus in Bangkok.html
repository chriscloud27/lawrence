    Introduction

    Wells International Kindergarten – Thong Lo Campus opened in 2003 and is situated at 6 Sukhumvit Soi 51, within walking distance of Thong Lo BTS station in Bangkok’s residential Thong Lo district. The campus offers an American‑based kindergarten curriculum for children aged 2 to 7, framed by the Thai Ministry of Education and Wells’ own standards. The layout centers around a child‑safe courtyard with slides, swings, and ample natural light, surrounded by classrooms equipped with technology for hands‑on learning. Specialist facilities include an art studio, music and dance studio, computer lab, and a small gymnasium, along with a swimming pool and bicycle track, supporting creative and active play . Headed by Gona Narula, this co‑educational, early‑years campus emphasizes a nurturing, play‑based environment aimed at fostering confidence and imagination in children aged 2–7 from a diverse international community.

    The Essentials

    Wells International School - Thong Lo Campus has 150 pupils, typical class sizes of 16, instruction in English.

    Location
    Wells International Kindergarten – Thong Lo Campus is in Bangkok’s Thong Lo neighbourhood, a short walk from the BTS station of the same name. The area is a mix of residential streets and small businesses, offering a calm setting within the city. The campus surrounds a central courtyard, giving children space to play and learn away from the busy main roads.

    Stages
    The campus serves children from Nursery through Grade 2, typically ages 2 to 7, focusing exclusively on early years education before students continue at other campuses for higher grades.

    Type
    This is a co‑educational, early‑years campus offering day schooling only. There are no boarding options available at this location.

    Additional learning support
    The school website does not publicly disclose information regarding specific provisions for Special Educational Needs (SEN) or Additional Learning Needs at this campus.

    Country affiliation
    The school is part of the EverClever Education Group, a Thai-based private operator, licensed by the Thai Ministry of Education. It does not maintain affiliation with any foreign government or national education system.

    Religious affiliation
    There is no religious affiliation; the campus operates on a secular basis and welcomes families of all backgrounds.

    School day structure
    The Thong Lo campus operates on weekdays, Monday through Friday, with classes running from 08:00 to 16:00. These timings are based on publicly listed operating hours from trusted directories, as the official website does not confirm the exact schedule. Break and lunch timings are not published online.

    Bus service
    A school-operated bus service is available, with routes organised by distance zones from the campus. Families can choose round-trip or one-way service, and pick-up and drop-off arrangements are coordinated directly with the school. The service is available to students in the Thong Lo campus age range and covers key residential areas in Bangkok.

    Fees

    Annual tuition at Wells International School - Thong Lo Campus ranges from €5,316 to €11,164 for 2026/27.

    Application Fees
    A one-time Campus Development Fee of €1,861 is required for new students (non-refundable).
    A refundable Damage Deposit of €797 is collected on admission and is returnable if claimed within two years of withdrawal.
    A Registration Fee of €120 applies starting from Kindergarten 3 (K3) and above.

    Tuition Fees (Per Semester)

    Nursery (N): €3,176
    Kindergarten 1 (K1): €3,442
    Kindergarten 2 (K2): €3,575
    Kindergarten 3 (K3): €3,735
    Grade 1 – Grade 2: €4,960

    Fees are billed twice per year (Semester 1 and Semester 2), with payment due before the start of each semester.

    Additional Fees
    Uniforms, certain materials, field trips, and extracurricular activities are billed separately.
    A mandatory lunch fee is charged each semester for all students.

    Transportation Fees (Per Semester)
    A school-operated bus service is available with both round-trip and one-way options, organised by distance zones:
    - Zone A: Round-trip – €691 / One-way – €505
    - Zone B: Round-trip – €797 / One-way – €585
    - Zone C: Round-trip – €890 / One-way – €665
    - Zone D: Round-trip – €997 / One-way – €731
    Routes and specific pick-up/drop-off points are confirmed with the transport office after enrolment.

    Learning Support
    When applicable, additional fees may be charged for ESL/Learning Support:
    - K2 – K3: €731 – €744
    - Grade 1 – Grade 2: €1,196 – €1,462

    Sibling Discount
    Concessions apply to families with multiple children:
    - Discounts on the Campus Development Fee for the 2nd child; further concessions for 3rd and 4th children.
    - Additional tuition discounts are offered for families with three or more enrolled children.

    Refund Policy
    The Damage Deposit is refundable upon withdrawal when claimed within the stipulated time frame (up to two years after withdrawal).
    Other refund conditions are defined in the school’s enrolment contract.

    Payment Options
    Payments can be made via bank transfer or at the school’s finance office.
    An advance payment discount is available for full-year tuition paid upfront.
    An instalment plan is offered (service charge applies); late payments may incur a daily fee.
    Academics

    Wells International School - Thong Lo Campus teaches American Curriculum for students aged 2 to 7.

    Curriculum
    Wells International Kindergarten – Thong Lo Campus follows an American-based curriculum adapted for early childhood education and licensed by the Thai Ministry of Education. It serves students from Nursery to Grade 2 (ages 2–7), focusing on literacy, numeracy, science, social studies, and creative arts. Learning is delivered through a combination of thematic units, hands-on activities, and structured play. The programme incorporates specialist subjects such as art, music, physical education, and ICT, with lessons designed to build foundational academic and social skills. Upon completing Grade 2, students transition to Wells’ On Nut or Bang Na campuses to continue in the American curriculum, which later offers Advanced Placement (AP) and the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IB DP).

    Admissions

    Admissions
    1. Apply to join the Wells community. Families may submit the Student Application Form either by visiting the campus in person or by downloading and emailing the completed form to the respective campus. Signatures must still be provided on-site for emailed submissions.

    2. Schedule a time for testing. Students applying for Grade 1 or 2 complete a paper-based assessment evaluating foundational academic skills. Those applying to Grades 3–12 undertake several components: DORA (Diagnostic Online Reading Assessment), ALEKS (math assessment), a writing sample, and a personal interview.

    3. Submit official documents. Once a student is accepted, families must provide documentation including passport copies (student and parents), recent academic reports, medical check-up records, ID photos, and any additional paperwork requested by the school. Thai nationals must also provide a house registration copy.

    4. Pay school fees. Before registration can proceed, families must pay all applicable fees such as tuition, enrollment, uniforms, and any additional charges for ESL or after-school programs as needed.

    5. Register for classes. Upon confirmation of payment, students will be issued class schedules, basic supplies, and a student-parent handbook. This step finalizes enrollment and allows students to begin attending classes.

    Scholarships
    Wells International School offers scholarships to new students, particularly at the secondary level, based on academics, sports, or the arts. Awards are decided by the school each year and applied directly to school fees. Applicants must submit documentation such as transcripts, portfolios or performance samples, letters of recommendation, and a personal profile, and may be interviewed by an administrator. The number and amount of scholarships awarded vary annually.

    Waitlist
    There is no mention on the school's official website of a waitlist or pooled enrollment system.
