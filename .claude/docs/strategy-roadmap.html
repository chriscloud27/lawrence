Lawrence: 5-Year Roadmap

2026–2031: From MVP through market leadership

Overview

This roadmap traces Lawrence from MVP (Sept 2026) through market leadership (Dec 2031), marking the inflection points where business model decisions compound into defensible moat and path to €50K/month personal income.

Key decision gate: Q3–Q4 2028, when you cross the $20K MRR concentration threshold (NRR >110%, churn <5%). This is where you choose: scale Lawrence full-time or keep it as one of two micro-SaaS products.

Product Scope

  • ✓ BANT pre-qualification (Stage 1: JS scoring, 0–75 pts)
  • ✓ n8n MVP (basic ingestion + Gmail handoff)
  • ✓ Supabase schema (leads, messages, schools)

Go-to-Market

  • Direct outreach to 3–5 UK/SE Asia education agencies (warm intros)
  • Pilot pricing: free or low-cost (€500–1K/month)
  • Success metric: 2+ agencies actively using, 1 paying, signal that BANT logic is useful

Biggest Risk

Product-market fit failure. The BANT pre-qual logic might not predict hot leads in practice. If pilot agencies report that scoring is no better than their workflow, the moat strategy collapses.

Mitigation: Run daily scorecards (which leads scored hot actually converted?). Iterate scoring weights monthly. Be prepared to pivot to "lead enrichment" if BANT doesn't work.

You're building this lean: part-time engineer, bootstrapped ops. Focus is validation, not scale. Revenue is secondary.

Product Scope (Critical Addition)

  • ✓ Outcome feedback loop (track lead closures; train model on outcomes)
  • ✓ Outcome-based pricing model (base + per-hot-lead fee)
  • ✓ Agency analytics dashboard (conversion trends)
  • ✓ Expanded geographic scope (UK + SE Asia)
  • ⊘ Multi-tenant architecture (stays single-agency; defer to Phase 3)

Three Biggest Risks

  1. NRR collapse. Agencies see churn, not growth. Mitigation: quarterly business reviews; tie pricing to agency revenue, not just lead count.
  2. Margin compression. LLM costs spike or you need GPT-4o instead of mini. Mitigation: build cost model now; contract with OpenAI early.
  3. Competitive entry. McKinsey/Deloitte launch an AI education tool. Mitigation: lock agencies with multi-year contracts by end of 2028.

Concentration Decision Gate (Q4 2028)

Measure three metrics at year-end:

  • NRR >110%? Agencies expanding + new logos offsetting churn
  • Churn <5%? Annual cohort retention healthy
  • Gross margin 65%+? Economics work at scale

If YES to all three: Lawrence has product-market fit. Commit to full concentration in 2029+. Shut down or spin down other projects.

If NO: Keep as "one of two micro-SaaS" (70–80% of your time). Optimize for profitability, not growth. Still pursue €50K/month total across both products.

(This roadmap assumes YES—the concentration path.)

Biggest Risk: Founder Burnout

You're now managing a real team and running feature roadmap planning. Operational overhead grows. If you haven't delegated product/ops by mid-2029, burnout is likely.

Mitigation: Hire a COO or general manager. You transition to founder/investor role, not day-to-day operator.

Key insight: At €50K MRR with 60%+ margins, you're now running a defensible vertical SaaS company. This is where the personal income target becomes achievable.

Phase 4 & 5 (2030–2031)

Phase 4 (2030): Scale to 50+ agencies, €80K MRR. School network launches. Parent app goes live. Network effects kick in (agencies + schools + parents all derive value). Second product incubation begins.

Phase 5 (2031): Lawrence at €100K+ MRR. Second product at early traction (€20K MRR). Combined company revenue €120K+. Personal income target achieved.

Stress Test vs. Ambition

Is €50K/month by Dec 2031 realistic?

AMBER Achievable if BANT works + NRR stays >110%. Requires: (1) product-market fit by Q4 2027, (2) 2.5x growth in 2029, (3) low churn maintained. One major setback (competitive entry, churn spike, cost explosion) delays to 2032.

Is $10M net worth achievable?

ORANGE Harder without exit or secondary purchase. €150K+ MRR by 2032 on a 5–6x multiple ≈ $7–9M valuation. With accumulated profits, you're close. But this requires both products scaling concurrently.

Can you stay concentrated on Lawrence?

GREEN Yes, by end of Phase 2 (Q4 2028). If metrics hit (NRR >110%, churn <5%), the data makes the decision for you.

Honest assessment: The €50K/month target by late 2031 is ambitious. More realistic: €30–40K by Dec 2031, €50K+ by mid-2032. But if BANT becomes a known framework and your moat solidifies early, you can compress the timeline.

Recommendation: Set €50K/month as the target, but plan for €35–40K by Dec 2031. The real milestone is proving the moat works (Phase 2). After that, the rest compounds.
